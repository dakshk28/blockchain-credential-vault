How it works

Issue

A verified university uploads the certificate. Its SHA-256 fingerprint is recorded and signed.

Store

The document is stored privately. Integrity monitoring detects any change to the stored file.

Share

The student shares the credential ID, QR code, or an expiring share link.

Verify

Anyone confirms status and authenticity. Every action is written to a tamper-evident ledger.
